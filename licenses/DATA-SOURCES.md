# Data sources and reuse

- Natural Earth boundaries and populated places: public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.
- Control-route overlays: Natural Earth 1:10-million land-boundary lines, downloaded 9 October 2026, https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land.geojson. Original names/classes/ids are retained. Generalised source snapshots are not live military positions or certified legal routes.
- mledoze/countries: Open Database Licence 1.0; derived country data is in data/atlas.json. The original licence is included.
- IPU Parline: Inter-Parliamentary Union, https://data.ipu.org/compare/, retrieved 8 October 2026. Derived data/legislatures.json and the legislature subset of data/topics.json are under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/. This dataset therefore has a noncommercial reuse condition. It is not covered by a blanket software licence.
- NobelPrize.org: official Nobel Prize API, https://api.nobelprize.org/v1/laureate.json. Names, dates, categories, birthplace and affiliation facts were selected; motivations, photographs and biographies are not reproduced. Data attribution/terms: https://data.nobelprize.org/specification/2.0/ and https://www.nobelprize.org/organization/developer-zone-2/.
- D3 7.9.0: ISC licence, included.
- Curated study summaries are educational editorial content with per-record reference links. Original source articles are not included. Modern country tags do not establish nationality, treaty-party status or ancient borders.
